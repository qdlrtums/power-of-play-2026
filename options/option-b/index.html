The session

Four stages, always in this order

Nothing about the room changes between them — only what the clinician is watching for.

  1. Free play

    Minutes 0–8 · no prompts

    The child chooses the materials. We record what gets picked up, what gets ignored, and how long attention holds unaided.

  2. Invitation

    Minutes 8–14 · one prompt per domain

    A single open-ended offer probes the domains that free play didn't surface on its own.

  3. Caregiver turn

    Minutes 14–20 · clinician steps back

    The caregiver plays; the clinician observes the pair. Serve-and-return is scored only here.

  4. Record and route

    Same day

    A scored profile with an explicit next step: continue, re-screen at a named age, or refer with evidence attached.

Who it's for

Built for the people already in the room

Pediatric clinics

Screening that fits a well-child visit, produces a chartable record, and gives you defensible grounds for a referral — or for holding off on one.

Early intervention teams

A shared vocabulary across OT, SLP, and behavioural staff, so a handoff carries the observation and not just the score.

Families

A plain-language summary of what the session showed, what it didn't, and what to look for at home before the next visit.
